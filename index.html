Lunar Farm

Run the farm that keeps a lava-tube colony alive. Drag out pressurised grow halls, sow them, and balance power, water, oxygen and carbon through a fourteen-day lunar night. The crop roster is drawn from plants that have actually flown.

About these games

Original code, familiar shapes

These are new games, written from scratch. They take their shape from the Maxis management sims of the early nineties — a map you build on, a tool palette, a simulation that answers back — but they share no code, art or data with them, and none of those games is distributed here. The originals are still someone's property; this is homage, not a repack.

Where a number could be grounded in real research it has been: the crop list in Lunar Farm is made of plants that have actually been grown in orbit or are long-standing candidates for bioregenerative life support, and the lunar day, the carbon loop and the power budget all behave roughly as they should. The exact values are tuned to make a good game, not to publish.

The other lunar games live in the Lunar Arcade, and the CoSE Arcade is the front door to everything — including Settlers of the Moon or Mars, Brute Bowl and the Lunar Red Alert conversion.
