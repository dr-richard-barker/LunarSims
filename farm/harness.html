Lunar Farm — simulation harness

Runs the simulation headlessly for hundreds of days and checks that the difficulty curve still behaves: that a careless farm fails, a competent one closes the food loop, and each modifier bites by the amount it claims to.

No DOM, no renderer — only data.js and sim.js, which is why this is possible at all. Math.random is replaced with a seeded generator so every run is reproducible. Re-run this page after any change to the simulation or its data.
